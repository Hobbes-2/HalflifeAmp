Halflife Amp

A portable amp made for the Hackclub Halflife! This amp is 100mm x 100mm total, including the case. It has three knobs, a female headphone jack, and a female aux jack. Its meant to be used with headphones or a aux speaker, as there is no built in speaker.

I was reading some books, as one does, and I was marveling at the various pieces of tech that people were carrying. Specifically, the "worse" of the such. Things like old battered phones, devices, the like. The cassette player from 20th Century Boys is what inspired me to do this particular project. (And the fact that I kept tripping on my headphones cord while playing my bass.) Its meant to have tape, stickers, and other such things on it - be imperfect. Its barely functional to be honest, but it works nonetheless, which personally i feel like adds to the character of the device. (Call me crazy)
